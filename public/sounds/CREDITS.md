# Sounds

Copied from Bodai Sudoku (sudoku.bodaijewels.com/sounds) on Nov 23, 2026, so both games
sound like one family.

The `family-1.m4a` to `family-12.m4a` laughs are Surbhi's own family recordings, cut to two
seconds from home videos and used with the family's permission. They play, shuffled with the
three recorded laughs below, when a ladder is solved.

All other sounds are from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under **CC0** (public domain:
free to use, no credit required). They are the site's 128 kbps MP3 previews, kept unedited;
volume and trimming are set in `SAMPLES` in `src/feedback/tones.ts`.
