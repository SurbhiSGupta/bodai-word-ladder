# Her and Frisky

The stick figure is Surbhi, and Frisky is the dog. They're the heart of every Bodai game, so they look the same everywhere.

<Canvas of={BuddyStories.Together} />

## How they're drawn

- **One pen.** Every line is the same ink color (`--ink`) at 2.6 units, with round ends and round joins. Eyebrows are thinner (1.8), the glasses thinner still (1.7).
- **She has no fill.** Just lines on the background, so she works on any surface and in both themes.
- **Glasses are the one color on her:** navy in light mode (`--glasses`), a soft periwinkle in dark mode so they still show.
- **Frisky is neon.** His fur is Bodai neon (`--fur`), his ear one shade deeper (`--fur-shade`), with a pink tongue (`--tongue`) and a red collar (`--collar`). He stays neon in every palette: he's a character, not a theme color.
- **Blush is optional,** for the cute, joy and kiss faces only (`--blush`, 75% opacity).

## Where they go

- Frisky sits at her feet, on the **left**, facing right.
- They stand on the same baseline. Never float them, crop their feet or put them inside a card.
- Keep them at least 120px tall on the home screen, so the faces read. Smaller than 64px, show Frisky alone.
- Nothing overlaps them except hearts, a ball or a leash from the story scenes.

## Faces and when to use them

| Face    | Use it for                                                             |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| default | Resting on the home screen                                             |
| happy   | Waving hello, a new puzzle                                             |
| cute    | Tapping her, the tour                                                  |
| curious | A hint, "what's that word?"                                            |
| joy     | Puzzle solved                                                          |
| kiss    | Under par, a new best                                                  |
| sad     | Running out of bones. Use sparingly, and never for a single wrong word |

## Outfits

The same as Bodai Sudoku's, and part of the same one-time Support pack (a Boduku code works here too).

- **Hers:** party hat, crown, beret, sunglasses, scarf, cape. Each is filled in its own `--wear-*` color with the same pen; the cape sits behind her.
- **Frisky's:** collar (as he is), bandana in his collar red, bow tie in scarf blue.
- One of each at a time. Without the pack they show only on the Support screen, as a try-on. `Buddies` takes `outfit` and `friskyOutfit`; left out, they follow `OutfitContext`.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolor her lines with the palette accent.
- Don't stretch, skew or outline them.
- Don't give her a speech bubble with long text. A short toast nearby works better.
- Don't use the sad face to make anyone feel bad.
