# Interaction rules

How the game responds to hands, keys and screen readers. These are design decisions, so they live here with the rest of the system.

## Keyboard

| Key                | What it does                              |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| A to Z             | Types into the current rung               |
| ← →                | Moves between letters in the current rung |
| Backspace          | Clears the letter to the left             |
| Delete             | Clears the selected letter                |
| Enter              | Adds the word as a rung                   |
| ⌘/Ctrl + Z         | Undo (steps one rung down)                |
| ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + Z | Redo                                      |
| ?                  | Hint (H can't be used: it's a letter)     |
| Space              | Pause, when a timer is running (Ninja)    |
| Escape             | Closes the open sheet or menu             |

Shortcuts never fire while a text field has focus, except Enter and Escape.

The 🔍 button looks up the word you've typed: whether it's in our list, and what it means. It's free in Zen; in Ninja the clock keeps running, so it's fair either way.

## Touch

- Every tap target is at least **44 × 44px**.
- The on-screen keyboard sits at the bottom, in the thumb zone.
- No long-press-only actions: anything a long press does has a visible button too.
- No swipes for core moves. Swipes are shortcuts only.

## Feedback

Every moment has one sound and one buzz, or deliberately none. See **Foundations → Sound and haptics** for the full list.

- **Real word:** the rung slides into place and two rising notes play.
- **Not a word:** the rung shakes (320ms), one bone drops into Frisky's mouth, and he gives a quiet bark.
- **Blocked word:** gentle toast ("Let's keep it friendly 🐾"). No shake, no sound, no bone.
- **Solved:** her joy face, a laugh (the family's twelve and three recorded ones, shuffled), a success buzz.
- **Under par:** Frisky barks first, then the laugh.

Recordings are in `public/sounds` with their credits, fetched the first time a sound plays.

## Screen readers

- Each rung is announced as a whole word ("Step: CORD"), never letter by letter.
- New rungs, errors and toasts are announced as they happen (`role="status"`).
- Bones are read as a sentence: "7 of 10 bones left".
- Green and yellow are never the only signal. The Rung's label and the end word say the same thing in words.

## Motion

- Default movement uses `--ease-standard` at `--duration-base` (220ms).
- Celebrations may use `--ease-bounce` and `--duration-celebrate`.
- With **reduced motion** on, nothing moves or shakes. Color changes and sounds stay, so no meaning is lost.

## Writing

- Plain, warm and short: "Not in our word list", "Under par! 🎉", "Frisky ate a bone".
- Name what the player sees, not how the game works.
- Never blame the player.
